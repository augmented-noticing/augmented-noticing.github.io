About these annotations

RQ1–RQ3 statements are researcher-generated analytic annotations grounded in public documentation and the verified literature corpus. They are not practitioner quotations, participant excerpts, or evidence of nonhuman intention. Public exposure is not treated as engagement, action, or lasting consequence.

Images are reproduced from creators' and venues' public pages for scholarly reference; credits and permission status are listed per work. Notes you add here stay in your browser until exported.
